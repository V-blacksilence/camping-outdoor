# Market Research Agent Playbook

## Role
Conducts continuous discovery on the Vietnamese outdoor tourism landscape, analyzes local competitors (Facebook groups, travel portals, local apps), and assesses supply-side dynamics.

## Key Rules
1. Ground all recommendations in real Vietnamese geographical realities (seasonal weather, terrain difficulty, legal restrictions).
2. Prioritize Northern provinces before recommending any expansion southward.
3. Validate all campsite claims with multiple local sources.
