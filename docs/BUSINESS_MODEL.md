# Business Model & Revenue Validation Strategy

## 1. Early Revenue Streams (MVP Phase)

Avoid building high-overhead payment gateways or inventory warehouses upfront. Focus on validated transactional commission:

1. **Campground Partner Booking Commissions (B2B2C)**:
   - Partner with glamping and organized campsites around Hanoi (Soc Son, Ba Vi, Dong Mo) and Hoa Binh.
   - Mechanism: App provides deep link or in-app inquiry form routing to campsite manager; earn an 8%–12% referral/booking fee upon confirmed stay.
2. **Outdoor Equipment Rental Commissions**:
   - Partner with existing rental shops in Hanoi.
   - Mechanism: The in-app packing checklist allows users to click "Rent from Partner"; earn a 10% affiliate commission on rental bundles.
3. **Featured Campsite Sponsorship (Pay-for-Placement)**:
   - Verified campgrounds pay a monthly listing fee (500,000 – 1,500,000 VND/month) to appear as "Featured Campsite of the Week" on the map and discovery carousel.

---

## 2. Unit Economics & Breakeven Targets
- Average weekend camping spend per person in Northern Vietnam: **350,000 – 1,200,000 VND**.
- Commission capture target per booking: **40,000 – 150,000 VND**.
- Target for Month 3 post-launch: 250 monthly completed bookings across 20 partner campsites in Northern Vietnam $\rightarrow$ **15,000,000 – 30,000,000 VND/month** gross margin, covering core cloud hosting and operational costs.

---

## 3. Long-Term Ecosystem Monetization
- **CampVN Pro Subscription**: High-resolution offline topographical maps, private member-only wild camping spots, emergency satellite rescue SOS coordination.
- **Brand Partnerships**: Sponsored gear reviews and gear testing campaigns with outdoor equipment manufacturers (Decathlon, Naturehike, Coleman).
